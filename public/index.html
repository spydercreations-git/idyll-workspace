Sales Executive at Idyll Productions

Join our creative team and help connect brands with world-class video production services. We're looking for driven, enthusiastic individuals ready to grow.

What You'll Be Doing
  • Create professional presentations (PPTs) and pitch decks for client meetings
  • Prepare detailed and compelling client proposals tailored to each prospect
  • Conduct timely follow-ups with potential and existing clients
  • Communicate effectively with leads across various channels
  • Make domestic as well as international calls when required
  • Explain Idyll Productions' services professionally and confidently to potential clients
  • Assist in building and maintaining strong client relationships
We are open to hiring people who are still learning sales and business development, provided they are willing to put in genuine effort and improve their communication skills. Training and guidance will be provided where necessary.
Compensation Structure
100% Commission
  • No fixed salary for the first 3 months — this is a commission-only opportunity.
  • You get paid only when you successfully bring a client that closes a project with Idyll Productions.
  • Your earnings are directly based on the business you generate for the company.
Commission Rate: 15% on every project you bring

If you do not bring any clients, there will be no payment during the commission-based period. Please understand this clearly before applying.
